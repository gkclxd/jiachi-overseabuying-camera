# Price Tag Camera for Personal Shoppers

A mobile web app for overseas personal shoppers that adds price tags to product photos as you shoot them. Set your shop name, break-even exchange rate, shipping fee and Japanese tax rate. Then open the camera and enter a JPY price. The app calculates your break-even cost and shows 8 quick selling prices (markup or exchange-rate based, rounded up to the nearest NT$10), each with its net profit. A white label with your shop name and TWD price appears on the live camera view. Shoot, then share the photo to a LINE chat in one tap.

## How to use

1. Open the GitHub Pages URL on your phone. The live camera requires HTTPS.
2. On the Settings screen, enter your shop name, break-even rate, shipping fee per item and tax rate.
3. Tap "開啟相機" (Open camera), enter the JPY price and pick a selling price. Drag the label to move it.
4. Tap the shutter, then "傳到 LINE" (Send to LINE), and choose a chat.

## Files

- `index.html`: the complete app in a single file. No server is needed, and settings are stored only in the phone's browser.

Note: the app interface is in Traditional Chinese.
